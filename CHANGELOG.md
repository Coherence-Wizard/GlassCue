# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Native transparent, always-on-top teleprompter for macOS 26 and Apple silicon.
- On-device speech following that holds position during digressions and resumes on matching phrases.
- Center-aligned text with adjustable font size, reading position, opacity, and background.
- Read-only live Markdown links, including Obsidian formatting cleanup.
- Click-through mode, global shortcuts, Escape dismissal, and a close button.
- Optional timed scrolling and a menu bar controller.

Validation: 22 automated checks passed, generated speech held position during an off-script digression and reacquired the script, and live microphone following was manually tested. Long-duration sessions and recording-output exclusion have not been verified.
